# Phase 6 — Section-by-section legal verification

**Status:** In progress
**Reviewed:** 27 August 2026

## Objective

Convert every syllabus topic into a traceable learning unit:

ICAB syllabus topic → governing Act/Rule/Notification → exact section/rule → study translation → plain-language explanation → Bangladesh example → exam focus.

## Verification order

1. Financial Reporting Act, 2015 + FRC Auditor & Audit Firm Enrollment Rules, 2022
2. Insurance Act, 2010
3. Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 + Labour Rules, 2015
4. Bank Company Act, 1991 + Financial Institutions Act, 1993
5. Finance Company Act, 2023
6. Securities & Exchange instruments
7. Companies Act, 1994 and Secretarial Practices

## Source hierarchy

Primary sources are the current ICAB syllabus/workbook, Bangladesh Laws, official Gazette, BSEC, Bangladesh Bank, FRC, IDRA and Ministry of Labour and Employment. Secondary legal databases may assist discovery but do not override a primary source.

## Important distinction

An Act section, Rule, BSEC notification, Bangladesh Bank circular and regulator guideline must be displayed as separate source types. A regulatory requirement that changes through a circular must not be presented as a permanent statutory provision.

## Financial Reporting Act — first verification pass

The ICAB syllabus expressly covers the Financial Reporting Act, 2015 and the Financial Reporting Council (Auditor & Audit Firm Enrollment) Rules, 2022. The syllabus gives this combined area 10% indicative weighting. The ICAB study manual identifies likely examination areas including establishment and structure of the Council, objectives, powers/functions, Executive Directors and Chairman, working divisions, auditor enlistment and cancellation, audit practices, reports/opinions, independence, standards, monitoring, complaints, appeals and rule-making.

The ICAB study manual also identifies section 2 definitions including financial year and financial statements. These should be retained as exact anchors and expanded only after checking the authentic Act text.

The 2024 ICAB Corporate Laws & Practices material reproduces the BSEC Financial Reporting and Disclosure Notification of 20 June 2018. That notification is a securities-regulatory instrument made under section 2CC of the Securities and Exchange Ordinance, 1969 and should remain separate from the Financial Reporting Act itself.

## QA rules

- Never invent a section number to fill a card.
- Never label a paraphrase as the verbatim Act text.
- Bangla text in the learning card is a study translation unless the authentic Bangla enactment is explicitly being reproduced.
- Numerical limits, deadlines, fees, ratios and eligibility criteria require amendment-status checking.
- If an exact provision cannot be verified, show `Verification required` rather than a false citation.
- Each completed topic should carry an official-source link.

## Completion label

A topic may be marked **Verified** only when its exact legal source is identified and checked. A module may be marked **Exam-ready** only when all of its syllabus topics meet that standard.
