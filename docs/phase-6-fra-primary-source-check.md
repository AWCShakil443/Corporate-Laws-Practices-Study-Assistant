# Phase 6 — FRA primary-source check

## Verified source position

This file records primary-source checks made before exposing FRA content as exact legal references.

### Financial Reporting Act, 2015
- Act: Financial Reporting Act, 2015.
- Section 31: public-interest entities must use an auditor enlisted by the Council; the current FRC auditor portal explicitly warns that an auditor not appearing on the enlisted list is prohibited from auditing PIEs under section 31 and that non-compliance is a criminal offence.
- Section 40: standards-setting is an important statutory anchor. The FRC-hosted material reproduces section 40(4), which permits the Council to set a separate simplified financial reporting structure and standards for small and medium enterprises while maintaining consistency with international best practice.
- Section 44: FRC-hosted material confirms that, from commencement, a public-interest entity subject to a legal financial-statement obligation must have its financial statements audited by an auditor enlisted by the Council and prepared following the applicable standards.

### Financial Reporting Council (Auditor and Audit Firm Enlistment) Rules, 2022
- FRC's official legislative-information page lists the Rules as published on 10 November 2022.
- The current FRC enlistment portal states that renewal is made under Rule 8 and that the application may be submitted online and offline with a copy of the enlistment certificate.
- The portal's current guidance states that an individual auditor applying for enlistment must be an ICAB or ICMAB member involved in audit services and that only one professional accountancy organisation membership may be used for an individual enlistment application.
- For an audit firm, the portal distinguishes sole proprietorship and partnership applications and identifies the proprietor or authorised/responsible partner as the applicant.

## UI publication rule

Only the above points should be marked VERIFIED from the primary-source check at this stage. Other Phase 6 topic mappings remain `Verification required` until their exact section/rule text has been checked against the primary source.

## Why this matters

The study assistant must distinguish:
1. statutory text;
2. rules/procedures made under the Act;
3. FRC administrative guidance; and
4. explanatory study material.

An administrative portal statement must not be presented as if it were the wording of the Act or Rules.
