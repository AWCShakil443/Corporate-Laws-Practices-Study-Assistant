# Phase 6 — Financial Reporting Act, 2015 verification

## Source basis
- ICAB Professional Level Corporate Laws & Practices syllabus: Financial Reporting Act, 2015 + Financial Reporting Council (Auditor & Audit Firm Enrollment) Rules, 2022, indicative weighting 10%.
- ICAB Corporate Laws & Practices study manual: examinable areas include FRC establishment/structure, objectives, powers and functions, Executive Directors/Chairman, working divisions, auditor enlistment and cancellation, audit practices, reports/opinions, auditor independence, standard setting/publication, monitoring of Public Interest Entity auditors, compliance with standards, complaints, appeals and rule-making.
- Primary legislation and rules must be checked before a topic is marked VERIFIED in the UI.

## Verification policy
A syllabus topic is not treated as an exact statutory provision merely because it sounds similar to a section heading. Each item must map to the actual section/rule and the current text. Where primary-source text is unavailable or amendment status is uncertain, the UI should show `Verification required`.

## Content rule
Box 1 must contain only verified statutory/rule text or a clearly labelled faithful study extract. Do not present an AI paraphrase as the "actual Act". Box 2 may explain the provision in simple language. Box 3 may provide a Bangladesh example. The exam box should identify the provision and the issue the candidate should address.

## Source note
The ICAB manual describes the FRA as a regulatory framework for financial reporting and auditing and identifies practical scenarios around FRC structure, auditor enlistment/cancellation, audit practice, independence, standards, monitoring, complaints, appeals and rule-making. The current ICAB syllabus confirms the FRA and 2022 Enrollment Rules as the LO5 content.
