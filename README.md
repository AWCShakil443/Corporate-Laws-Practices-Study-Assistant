# Corporate Laws & Practices Study Assistant

Single page ICAB Professional Level study interface.

## UX

- Three-column learning flow: fixed Act column → Learning Objectives/topic column → study content.
- No page navigation for normal study.
- Box 1: section/rule reference with English/Bangla view.
- Box 2: easy understanding.
- Box 3: Bangladesh scenario and Professional Level exam tip.
- Eye-smoothing palette using warm off-white, sage and muted green tones.
- Search, font controls and responsive mobile layout.

## Syllabus basis

The ICAB 2026 Corporate Laws and Practices workbook contains seven weighted syllabus areas: Companies Act and secretarial practices 30%, securities and exchange 20%, Financial Reporting Act/FRC Rules 10%, Bank Company Act 15%, Finance Company Act 5%, Insurance Act 10%, and Bangladesh Labour Act/Rules 10%.

## Important

The interface is a study aid. The authentic law, current amendments, Gazette notifications, BSEC rules and Bangladesh Bank/FRC directions remain authoritative. Where a topic does not yet have a curated section excerpt in the interface, the page directs the learner to the authentic source and provides a structured study framework rather than inventing statutory text.
